Linqi (临期本) Privacy Policy

Developer: Chongqing Huanshuo Network Technology Co., Ltd. (重庆环烁网络科技有限公司) · Effective date: 2026-10-29 · Last updated: 2026-10-29

Everything you record in Linqi is stored on your own iPhone.

1. We collect no data

  • Fully offline. Linqi does not connect to any server of ours (we don't have one), and the app makes no network requests.
  • No sign-up. There are no accounts. We never ask for a phone number, email, or any identity information.
  • Nothing collected. Item names, production dates, shelf life, expiry dates, locations and categories, reminder settings, photos, and barcodes are stored on your device and are never sent to us or any third party.
  • No ads or tracking. No advertising, analytics, tracking, or other third-party SDKs. We don't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and never ask for permission to track you.

2. Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only to capture production dates, shelf life, and barcodes on packaging.
  • Text and barcode recognition run entirely on your phone; images are never sent to us.
  • Item photos you choose to keep are stored in the app's on-device storage and are never sent to us.
  • When you pick from your photo library, only the photo you select is read.
  • You can turn off camera or photo access in iOS Settings at any time and still record items manually.

3. Reminders

Expiry reminders use iPhone local notifications. They are scheduled on your phone, never pass through a server, and don't use push services. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

4. Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read data on your device and never go online.

5. Payment (one-time purchase)

  • The full version is unlocked through Apple In-App Purchase, and Apple handles the payment.
  • We never receive your card, Apple ID, or other payment details.
  • Restore Purchases checks your purchase history through Apple's system APIs, not through any server of ours.
  • Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

6. Export, backup, and deletion

  • CSV export: exported files go only where you choose (for example the Files app or a share target you pick). What happens to them afterwards is up to you.
  • CSV import: only the file you select is read.
  • Device backup: all of Linqi's data (your item records, reminder settings, and the package photos you take) is stored on your device. The app never uploads it to any server and does not use iCloud sync. If you turn on iCloud Backup in iOS, or back up your iPhone to a computer, this data is included in that device backup and stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.
  • Deletion: deleting an item, or the app, removes the data from your device. We keep no copy, so we can't restore it for you.

7. Crash reports

Linqi does not include any third-party crash reporting, analytics, or advertising tools, and the app itself does not collect or upload crash logs. If you choose "Share With App Developers" in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide us with crash diagnostics that do not identify you, used only to fix problems. You can turn this off in that setting at any time.

8. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the in-app page and the "Last updated" date above; significant changes will be announced in the app.
